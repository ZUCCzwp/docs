## Who should apply

- Creators on **Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, YouTube, Twitter/X, Telegram, WeChat**, and similar channels
- Developer community leads, open-source maintainers, and technical bloggers
- Integrators who recommend ViraltokAI Open API to customers

## Before you apply

1. Register at the [ViraltokAI console](https://api.viraltok.ai/register)
2. Read the [API reference](/en/api-reference/introduction) and [model pricing](/en/api-reference/common/model-catalog)
3. Prepare **social profile URLs** and **account proof screenshots**

<Info>
Rewards are paid in **JimiCoin**. After transfer, they can be used for all Open API products (video, image, LLM, etc.). Rates and recharge counts depend on your **affiliate tier** assigned at review—always follow the console.
</Info>

The **affiliate program** is for creators and developers who recommend ViraltokAI to their audience. After approval, you receive a personal invite link and poster; when friends register via your link and recharge JimiCoin, you earn **inviter rewards** on their first N recharges.

<Card title="Apply in the console" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://api.viraltok.ai/console?tab=affiliate">
  Console → **Affiliate program** tab
</Card>

---

## Rules at a glance

| Item | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Invite limit** | No cap on invitees |
| **Reward trigger** | Each successful JimiCoin recharge by an invitee |
| **Recharge count** | First **N** recharges per invitee (default **3**, per your tier) |
| **Reward amount** | Invitee recharge JimiCoin × your **inviter rate** (%) from your affiliate tier |
| **Payout** | Credited to **transferable balance**; manually transfer to JimiCoin for API use |
| **Invite link** | `https://api.viraltok.ai/register?invite=<your_invite_code>` |

<Warning>
Rates, recharge counts, and tiers are set at review and may differ between affiliates. Do not promise fixed rates publicly—follow your console after approval.
</Warning>

## How to apply

Open [Console · Affiliate](https://api.viraltok.ai/console?tab=affiliate) and submit:

<Steps>
<Step title="Select promotion channels (multi-select)">
  Options include **Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, YouTube, Twitter/X, Telegram, WeChat, Other**. Pick channels you actually operate.
</Step>

<Step title="Social profile links">
  At least one reachable profile URL (e.g. `https://…`) to verify your reach.
</Step>

<Step title="Promotion plan">
  Describe how you will introduce ViraltokAI (see template below).
</Step>

<Step title="Account proof screenshot">
  Backend stats, follower metrics, or representative content proving you operate the account.
</Step>

<Step title="Contact information">
  Email, Telegram, WeChat, etc., for review follow-up.
</Step>

<Step title="Submit and wait">
  While status is **pending**, wait for review. When approved, you are notified by email (if available) and the console shows **approved**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Writing your promotion plan

Reviewers look for **audience fit**, a **credible execution plan**, and **product understanding**.

### Include

1. **Audience** — developers, short-video creators, ecommerce teams, etc.
2. **Channel and format** — tutorials, reviews, live demos, community Q&A, open-source demos
3. **Focus** — e.g. Sora/Seedance video API, GPT Image, LLM (Codex integration)
4. **Cadence** — weekly posts, series, campaign window
5. **Compliance** — pay-as-you-go billing, JimiCoin top-up required

### Example (adapt freely)

```text
I run a Bilibili channel and a Telegram developer group focused on indie builders.
Over the next 4 weeks I will publish 2 ViraltokAI tutorials: Open API video generation
with task polling, and Codex + ViraltokAI LLM via the Responses API, each with a GitHub demo.
I will answer questions in the group and clearly state pay-as-you-go pricing with links to official docs.
```

### Avoid

- Empty or one-line plans
- Guaranteed fixed rebate rates or “easy money” claims
- Plans that do not match selected channels

## After approval

1. Copy your **invite link** or download the **invite poster**
2. Have friends **register** through the link (invite code applied)
3. When they **recharge JimiCoin**, you earn rewards on their first N recharges
4. Review **reward records** in the console
5. **Transfer** transferable balance to JimiCoin for API usage

### Transfer rewards

Rewards are not auto-applied to your spending balance. Use **Transfer to account balance** in the console to move transferable balance into JimiCoin.

---

## Affiliate tiers

The platform assigns **affiliate tiers** (e.g. Starter, Trainee, Ambassador) based on reach and plan quality. Each tier defines:

- **Inviter reward rate** (%)
- **Max recharges per invitee** (first N)

Your tier is set at approval and may be adjusted later. Names and rates are shown in the console.

## Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Total invitees | Users registered with your invite code |
| Total recharge | Sum of invitee JimiCoin recharges |
| Total rewards | Your cumulative inviter rewards |
| Transferable balance | Rewards not yet transferred to spending balance |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can anyone apply?">
  You need real, verifiable channels and a concrete plan. Low-quality or fraudulent applications may be rejected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Must invitees use my link?">
  Yes. Only registrations with your invite code count toward your referrals and rewards.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the reward rate fixed?">
  No. It comes from your assigned tier and may change if your tier is updated. Follow the console.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a reward on the 4th recharge?">
  By default, only the first **3** recharges per invitee. If your tier sets a different N, follow the console.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I withdraw rewards to a bank account?">
  Rewards transfer to **JimiCoin** for Open API usage. Direct bank withdrawal is not supported.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Promotion guidelines">
  Describe pay-as-you-go billing accurately. Use the [public API docs](/en/api-reference/introduction) and [model pricing](/en/api-reference/common/model-catalog) in your content.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
